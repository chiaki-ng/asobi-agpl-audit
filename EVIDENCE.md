## Sony client comparison

[Windows client check](evidence/sony-windows-client.json): Sony PS Remote Play for Windows 9.5.0.08040, the client the developer named as his main source, from Sony's public installer. It has Sony's NP signaling and stun.playstation.net, and none of the chiaki-ng choices below. A heuristic x64 key-position search found no match.

[macOS client check](evidence/sony-mac-client.json): Sony PS Remote Play for Mac 9.5.0; none of the Chiaki markers.

[Three-way comparison](evidence/sony-three-way.json) of Sony PS Remote Play 9.5.0, chiaki-ng (pinned `4c2643f9`) and Asobi. Sony uses NP signaling, `stun.playstation.net` and a ring-buffer key position; Asobi uses none of these. Asobi carries chiaki-ng choices that Sony lacks: the always-online-stun list, the three-response port-increment cases, 75/8 port guesses, `size + size % 16` and the moonlight-first STUN list. Session messages common to all three are treated as protocol. The ACCEPT wait and Senkusha fallback were not comparable. URLs and constants are evidence of copying, not standalone protected expression.

## Dated publisher evidence

October 6 observations refer to [the publisher site](https://asobiapp.com/) and [its legal page](https://asobiapp.com/legal.html), plus supplied About/legal screenshots. The About view identifies 1.5 without a build number. Bulk page captures and private correspondence are withheld here; absence of visible notices is a bounded observer report, not a complete UI inspection. The developer's independent-implementation position and prior private-inspection offer are acknowledged in FINDINGS.md.

PSStream/Pylux strings and step labels remain separate Asobi provenance context. The compared public Pylux snapshot postdates the early Asobi release. On October 8 the historical PSStream build was checked: PSStream 2.9.8, uploaded to Steam on 2026-01-14 (BuildID 21499345), contains all 16 compared step labels and all eight identifiers. It also contains three authored log lines (`=== Kamaji Step 0.5c Response ===`, `=== Centralized Authorization Check ===`, `Detected platform from Kamaji session:`) that every examined Asobi release from 1.1 through 1.5 carries verbatim, after the word “PSStream” was removed in 1.1.9 and 1.2. An extended scan found one additional selected line first in 1.1.2 and seven first in 1.1.5; all eleven are in 1.5. None of these lines appears in seven pre-cloud releases. A Sony PS Remote Play iOS package has no exact selected-line hits, but it is not a cloud-client control; an official PlayStation Plus PC installer was extracted: none of the eleven selected lines or checked step markers occurs as exact ASCII across 140 client files, while all eight compared identifiers occur in `pspluslauncher.exe` and are excluded from PSStream-specific inference ([control data](evidence/psstream-steam-build.json)). This PS4/classic streaming PC client is not identical to the PS5 cloud service. Shared log wording does not establish how much PSStream source expression, if any, was translated. A Mac App Store copy of Asobi 1.1.5, bought by Forward Technologies, lists all 41 Chiaki/PSStream lines of iOS 1.1.5, including the four chiaki-ng source citations; its executable hash is their statement until the app is supplied. PSStream was sold on Steam from 2025-11-02. Its discontinuation was announced on 2026-01-16; the project continues as Pylux under AGPL-3.0. It was publicly sold before Asobi 1.1 (packaged 2026-02-13), the first release carrying PSStream markers. Forward Technologies has authorized pursuit of its rights in this Asobi case; that authorization does not independently establish the technical derivation claim. This curated package omits the underlying bulk string collection and cannot independently establish that separate claim from its summary alone.
